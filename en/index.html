1. Overview

Pausepoint is an app that helps you deliberately limit your own screen time. The app processes all data exclusively on your device. There is no server, no user accounts, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics services. No personal data is transmitted to the provider or to third parties. The app does not request the internet permission.

2. What data the app processes

Stored exclusively on the device:

  • the apps you select, along with their limits, schedules and settings;
  • usage times of the selected apps, which the operating system provides through usage access;
  • your streak (points, unlocked content) and your daily goals;
  • a randomly generated installation identifier (UUID) with no connection to your person.

This data does not leave your device.

3. Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

The app needs usage access in order to detect which app is in the foreground and to measure usage duration. This evaluation takes place entirely on the device. No usage data is transmitted.

4. Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Used to show the pause overlay over other apps.

5. Notification access (optional)

If you grant it, the app uses notification access solely to pause the media playback of the blocked app during a break. Notification content is not read, stored or transmitted. The feature is optional and can be revoked at any time in the Android settings.

6. Local backup file

In addition to the Android backup, the app writes a file to Downloads/PausepointBackup/state.json. It contains your blocked apps, limits, the streak, the retention period for goals and the installation identifier. Its purpose is to restore your progress after a reinstall. The file sits unencrypted in your public Downloads folder and can be read and deleted by you and by other apps with file access. It holds no passwords, no account details and no content from other apps.

7. Android system backup

Android itself also backs up the app's settings and database to your Google account as part of the device backup and transfers them to a new device. This process is controlled by Android, not by Pausepoint. You can disable it in the Android settings under "Backup".

8. Feedback function

When you send feedback through the app, the app opens your email app with a pre-filled message to pausepoint.feedback@gmail.com. The message is only sent once you confirm this in your email app. It contains your text, the app version and general device details (e.g. model, Android version) as well as — only if you enter it — your contact email. A copy is stored locally on the device. These emails are used solely to handle your request and are deleted afterwards.

9. Disclosure to third parties

No disclosure takes place. The app does not embed any advertising, analytics or social media SDKs and does not establish any network connections itself.

10. Children

The app is aimed at adults and is not intended for children. No data from children is knowingly collected.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since no data is stored by the provider or by third parties, there is nothing there to hand over, correct or delete. You can delete all locally stored data by clearing the app data in the Android settings or by uninstalling the app; additionally delete the file Downloads/PausepointBackup/state.json, if present.
